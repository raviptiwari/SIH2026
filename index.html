FROM FIELD TO PAYMENT

How it works

A clear six-step journey keeps every visit predictable.

  • 1
    Register
    Name, mobile number, farmer ID and crop details.
  • 2
    Book a slot
    Pick a procurement centre, date and time.
  • 3
    Get a token
    A queue number and a QR code for check-in.
  • 4
    Track the queue live
    See how many farmers are ahead and the expected wait.
  • 5
    Sell your crop
    Reach the centre when your turn is close, then complete procurement.
  • 6
    Get paid
    Track payment status until it's credited.
